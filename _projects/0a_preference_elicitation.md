**Independent research, ongoing**

Multi-agent LLM systems usually combine their agents' answers by simple majority vote. I am exploring how eliciting more information from the agents at inference time, and aggregating it with ideas from social choice, can help these systems reach better collective answers.

Ongoing work. More details coming soon.
